Why it's a primitive

One registry.
Every credit product.

A registry only one app reads is plumbing. So Hana ships two — deliberately different shapes of credit, drawing on the same imported score, with zero coupling between them.

Reference app #1
Retail BNPL
A shopper splits a purchase into instalments, funded by a real lending pool and settled through a vault.

Reference app #2
DePIN credit line
A node operator draws SPACE, auto-stakes it, and repays from staking yield instead of their own capital.
SPACE
