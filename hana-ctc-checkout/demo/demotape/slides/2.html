Why it's still unsolved

Moving the history is easy.
Trusting it isn't.

01
Just sign a message claiming it
A signature proves you hold a key. It proves nothing about what that key ever did.
02
Bridge it
A bridge moves assets and asks you to trust a validator committee. Credit isn't an asset.
03
Post it with an oracle
That reintroduces exactly the intermediary the protocol was supposed to remove.

And even once it's portable, Creditcoin has no permissionless, EVM-native place to put the answer — nothing an arbitrary contract can just read.
